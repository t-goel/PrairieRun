Everything you need to stay on track

📚

Multi-Source Scanning

Pulls assignments directly from PrairieLearn, Canvas (via calendar feed & planner), Gradescope, PrairieTest reservations, and custom course schedule pages.

📅

1-Click iCal Feed

Subscribe once in Apple Calendar, Google Calendar, Outlook, or mobile with zero complex OAuth setups. Updates automatically in the background.

🔒

Privacy-First Architecture

All credentials and course session cookies stay securely inside your browser. No personal assignment data is ever sent to third-party tracking servers.

🎯

Smart Keyword Filters

Easily exclude optional readings, surveys, or specific assignment keywords so your calendar only shows the work you actually care about.

🔗

Direct Course Links

One-click navigation straight into your course instance assessment portals. Paired PrairieLearn and PrairieTest shortcuts ready at a glance.

🌐

Cross-Browser Support

Runs on Chrome, Brave, Chromium-based browsers, and Firefox (including ESR 115+) with native platform storage and performance.
