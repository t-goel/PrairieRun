About PrairieRun

Built by UIUC students, for UIUC students.

At the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (UIUC), academic life is fragmented across a dozen disparate platforms. One course uses PrairieLearn for homework; another uses Gradescope for quizzes, Canvas for discussions, PrairieTest for proctored CBTF reservations, and a self-hosted course webpage for project milestones.

Keeping track of due dates manually leads to missed assignments, calendar clutter, and unnecessary stress. PrairieRun solves this problem by bringing all your deadlines together into one clean, consolidated, automatically-synced schedule.

How It Works

PrairieRun operates seamlessly as a browser extension:

  1. Automatic Extraction: When you browse your course portals, PrairieRun scans enrolled courses and upcoming assignments using your existing authenticated session.
  2. Normalization & Deduplication: Deadlines from different sources for the same class (like an assignment listed on both Gradescope and a course syllabus page) are unified into a single canonical entry.
  3. Calendar Synchronization: Eligible assignments are automatically published as calendar events—either directly to Google Calendar or through an anonymous, auto-updating iCal feed that works with Apple Calendar, Outlook, and Google Calendar.

Core Principles

  • Zero Data Selling or Tracking: We do not collect telemetry, track browsing behavior, or monetize user data. All assignment parsing runs locally in your browser.
  • Open Source & Auditable: Every line of code is open source and available for review on GitHub.
  • No Setup Hassle: With our one-click iCal feed, students can subscribe to their calendar without registering developer credentials or navigating Google Cloud Console.

Get Involved

PrairieRun is community-maintained and welcomes contributions, bug reports, and suggestions. Visit our GitHub repository to view the source code or get involved.
