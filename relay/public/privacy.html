Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

PrairieRun ("we", "us", or "our") is an open-source browser extension designed to help students track and organize course assignments. We believe in privacy by design: your academic records, grades, and credentials belong to you and should remain on your own device.

1. Information We Process

PrairieRun processes the following data exclusively to provide calendar synchronization and assignment digest features:

  • Course Data: Course codes, course names, and assessment links from services you are signed into (PrairieLearn, Canvas, Gradescope, PrairieTest, and user-specified course websites).
  • Assignment Information: Assignment titles, due dates, duration windows, and completion status.
  • User Preferences: Notification lead times, excluded keywords, custom course website URLs, and calendar view settings.

2. Where Data Is Stored

100% Local Storage: All parsed assignments, course links, settings, and credentials are stored strictly in your browser's local extension storage (chrome.storage.local / browser.storage.local).

We do not maintain user accounts on external servers for extension usage, and we do not operate telemetry, tracking, analytics, or behavioral profiling.

3. Calendar Integration & External Services

A. Hosted iCalendar Feed (Optional)

If you enable the anonymous iCal feed option, the extension creates an ICS calendar file containing your upcoming assignment titles and due dates. This file is published to the PrairieRun relay hosted on Vercel Blob storage at an unguessable URL derived from a randomized secret identifier (feedId).

No personal identity, email address, student ID, or password is attached to or stored in this feed. You can disable or discard this feed at any time from the extension dashboard.

B. Direct Google Calendar Sync (Optional)

If you choose to connect Google Calendar, PrairieRun uses Google OAuth 2.0 to request permission to create and manage dedicated calendars for your courses. Authentication tokens are kept in temporary session memory and are never transmitted to our servers or stored in persistent plain text.

4. Permissions Used by the Extension

  • storage: To persist your assignments, course links, and settings locally on your machine.
  • alarms: To schedule periodic local checks for assignment deadline updates.
  • identity: To support optional Google Calendar sign-in via Chrome/Firefox web auth flows.
  • host_permissions: Limited to accessing university course systems (e.g. https://us.prairielearn.com/*, https://canvas.illinois.edu/*, https://www.gradescope.com/*) only to read assignment tables and deadlines with your existing active session.

5. Google API Services User Data Policy

PrairieRun's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

You have complete control over your data:

  • You can remove individual assignments or hide them from calendar sync directly in the dashboard.
  • Uninstalling the PrairieRun extension automatically erases all locally stored assignments, settings, and cached tokens.

7. Open Source Code

PrairieRun is completely open source. You can audit the codebase, review network requests, and verify our privacy practices directly on our GitHub repository.

8. Contact

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please open an issue on GitHub at github.com/t-goel/PrairieRun/issues.
